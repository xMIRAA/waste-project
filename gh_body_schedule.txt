File: admin/manage_schedule.php and resident/resident_home.php
Severity: Major

Description:
The collection schedule flows are incomplete and potentially leaking information: there are no admin update/delete handlers for `pickup_schedule`, and the resident-facing `resident_home.php` shows the next schedule without filtering by user area. The `users` table also lacks an `area`/`zone` field so there's no way to map a resident to a schedule area.

Reproduction/Impact:
- Residents see upcoming schedules for other areas, leaking operational information and causing confusion.
- Admins cannot edit or remove schedule entries; fixing this may encourage ad-hoc insecure code.

Suggested fix:
- Add `area` or `zone` mapping to user profiles (DB migration), require area when creating users or provide admin assignment.
- Add admin UPDATE/DELETE handlers with prepared statements and existence checks.
- Filter resident schedule queries by the resident's assigned area.
